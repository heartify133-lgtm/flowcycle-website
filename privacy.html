Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2025

1. Our Privacy Philosophy

At FlowCycle Monitor, we believe your health data is yours alone. Unlike traditional period trackers, we do not follow a "data collection" business model. Our application is built on the principle of Zero-Knowledge.

The Core Promise: We do not have servers that store your health data. All information you input into FlowCycle Monitor stays on your physical device in an encrypted sandbox.

2. Information Collection

Personal Health Data: We do not collect, transmit, or store your cycle dates, symptoms, or moods on any external servers. This data remains strictly in your device's local storage.

Accounts: FlowCycle Monitor does not require you to create an account or provide an email address. Your usage is entirely anonymous.

AI Features: When you use our AI Insight features, your data is processed locally or via secure, anonymous API calls that do not link your identity to the data being analyzed. We do not use your data to train global AI models.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following services for specific functionality:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): To support the free version of the app. This may use an anonymous advertising ID but does not have access to your health data.
  • Firebase: Used for crash reporting and app performance monitoring to ensure the app works correctly.

4. Data Security

Because your data is stored locally, its security depends on your device security (e.g., your phone's passcode or biometric lock). We recommend using your phone's built-in security features to protect your local data.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
